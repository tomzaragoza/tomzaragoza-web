# Launch your first campaign

Instructional draft implemented at `/x-ads/launch`. The lesson copy is stored in `app/x-ads/course-data.ts`.

Based on Tom's instructions. Numbered steps describe the actions. Inset notes explain the reasoning.

## Lesson sequence

1. Start a Sales campaign.
2. Build a Europe / English ad group and set its budget and schedule.
3. Add follower look-alikes with Optimized Targeting enabled.
4. Select and verify the Purchase conversion.
5. Add other ad groups, keeping comparisons consistent.
6. Write natural ads that identify the problem and show the product's value.
7. Demonstrate the product in a video and check its website destination.
8. Add and verify a unique trackable link for each ad.
9. Test the landing page and signup flow, then review the campaign settings.
